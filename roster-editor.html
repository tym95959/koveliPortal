2. Officer Auto Roster Rules

Auto-saved locally
Simple setup: use the dropdowns below. Auto Create applies only to staff with roster role Officers. Other setup functions can be used for all included staff. Annual Leave overrides Weekly OFF when both fall on the same day. Group A and Group B should not share the same exact duty where staffing allows. Special Group must be represented on both Morning and Afternoon when enough Special members are available. Daily Officer requirement: exactly 2 × 1630, 1 × 0030 and 2 × 0830 whenever enough available Officers exist. Officers are not auto-assigned 1730. After OFF, Afternoon is preferred. The generator prefers consecutive Morning blocks and consecutive Afternoon blocks instead of alternating Morning/Afternoon.
